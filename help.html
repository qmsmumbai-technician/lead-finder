1. What is Lead Finder?

Lead Finder finds businesses and service providers on Google for any topic and area you choose, and gives you their name, address, phone number, website and map location.

What it is used for

  • My Kaarigar: finding plumbers, electricians, AC technicians and other skilled people to invite onto the app.
  • Let Us Care: finding carers, nursing bureaus, babysitting agencies and physiotherapists.
  • Anything else: pharma distributors, CA firms, schools, shops, in India and 14 other countries.

The 4 parts of the screen

Everything is stored only on this phone's browser. Nothing is sent to any server of ours.

2. How it works inside

  1. You choose a trade (e.g. Plumber) and an area (e.g. Nallasopara).
  2. Lead Finder asks Google Places, the same data as Google Maps, for "plumber in Nallasopara".
  3. Google returns up to 20 businesses per page.
  4. Lead Finder removes closed businesses, duplicates and (if you choose) numbers that are not mobiles.
  5. The rest are saved in the Provider database on your phone.
  6. You invite them on WhatsApp, and track who replied and who registered.

Where the data comes from

Only from Google's official business listings: businesses that put themselves on Google Maps. Lead Finder does not scrape websites or collect private people's data.

Google data can be old or wrong. Some numbers may be switched off or belong to someone else. Always be polite in the first message.

3. One-time setup: the Google key

Lead Finder needs a Google Places API key. This is already done for Abode Digital Technology in the Google Cloud project Lead Finder.

If you ever need a new key

  1. Open console.cloud.google.com. Use Desktop site in Chrome.
  2. Select the project Lead Finder, not Kaarigar or Firebase projects.
  3. ☰ → APIs & Services → Library → Places API (New) → Enable.
  4. ☰ → APIs & Services → Credentials → + Create credentials → API key.
  5. Restrict it: Websites → qmsmumbai-technician.github.io/*, and API → Places API (New) only.
  6. Paste it in Lead Finder → ⚙️ Settings → first box → Save settings.
Never share the key on WhatsApp or post it publicly. Because of the website restriction, it only works on your GitHub site.
Never add billing to the Kaarigar or Let Us Care Firebase projects. They stay on the free plan. Billing is only on Lead Finder.

How many calls a search uses

Trades × Areas × Pages. Example: 2 trades × 3 areas × 1 page (20 results) = 6 calls.

The app asks you to confirm any search over 10 calls.

After the 90-day trial

  • The account pauses and is not charged automatically.
  • To continue, you click Upgrade yourself. The 1,000 free calls a month continue after that.
Stay under about 30 calls a day and Lead Finder costs nothing. Set a ₹500 budget alert: Google Cloud ☰ → Billing → Budgets & alerts.

Check usage: Google Cloud ☰ → Google Maps Platform → Metrics.

5. Searching step by step

Choose a tab

TabUse it for
My KaarigarTechnicians. Ready WhatsApp invite + import lines
Let Us CareCarers and care agencies. Ready invite
Free searchAny topic. Plain WhatsApp button only

Then

  1. Tick trades (Kaarigar / Let Us Care), or type a Topic and Region (Free search).
  2. Areas: separate with commas, e.g. Nallasopara, Vasai, Virar.
  3. Country: India by default.
  4. Results per search: 20 (1 call), 40 (2 calls) or 60 (3 calls).
  5. Keep only: Mobile numbers (best for WhatsApp), Any phone, or Everything.
  6. Tap Search. A line like "Done. 19 new providers added…" appears.
Test a new trade in one area first. Rare trades (e.g. Vastu Consultant) need bigger areas like Vasai or Virar.

6. Areas and countries

Any area in India

  • City, town, neighbourhood or district: Andheri West, Thane, Pune, Surat.
  • Add the city to common names: Shivaji Nagar, Pune.
  • Split big cities into areas (each search returns at most 60): Andheri, Borivali, Dadar, not just "Mumbai".
  • Small villages have few listings, so search the nearest town.

15 countries

India, United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Singapore, Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka.

  • Dubai is in the UAE: choose UAE and type Dubai (or Deira, Bur Dubai, Al Barsha).
  • Outside India, Google can't tell mobiles from landlines, so all full numbers are kept. Check WhatsApp before relying on a number.

7. The Provider database

Every saved lead appears as a card showing name, app, trade, Google type, rating, area, address and phone.

Filters

Filter by app, status and trade, sort by Newest / AI score / Rating / Name, or type in the search box. The grey chips show counts for each status.

A business found under two trades keeps both trades (maximum 2, like the apps).

8. Status and WhatsApp outreach

StatusMeaning

InterestedReplied positively

Not interestedSaid no
Wrong numberNumber doesn't belong to this business

Good practice

  • Send one by one, about 5–10 a day, never a bulk blast. Too many messages to strangers can get your WhatsApp number blocked.
  • Use your WhatsApp Business number.
  • Every message ends with "Reply STOP…". If they reply STOP, tap Remove.
  • Change the message in ⚙️ Settings. You can use {name}, {trade}, {area}.

9. Getting providers into My Kaarigar

Never import people who haven't agreed. Only Interested or Registered providers.
  1. Invite on WhatsApp. When they agree, set status to Interested.
  2. Best: they install the app and register themselves, with reverse-WhatsApp verification as usual.
  3. If you want to add them yourself: Provider database → filter app My Kaarigar → tap 📥 Copy import lines.
  4. Open the Kaarigar admin panel → Import, paste, check the list, then add.

Import line format

Name, Trade, 10-digit mobile, Area, lat, lng

The lat, lng gives the correct "~km" distance in the customer app, even outside your area list.

Trade names in ⚙️ Settings must match the app's trade names. The Import check flags any that don't.

10. Using it for Let Us Care

  • Google has few individual carers. You will mostly find nursing bureaus, babysitting agencies, japa-maid services and physio clinics.
  • One agency can bring many carers, so talk to agencies first.
  • The Let Us Care invite says register free (the app takes no money).
  • Carers still register themselves and verify by WhatsApp. Families check ID themselves, as in the app.

Services searched

Toddler Care, Elder Care, Patient Care, Mother and newborn care, Special needs care, Physiotherapy at home. Edit the list and search words in ⚙️ Settings.

12. AI scoring and Agent mode

Both need a Claude API key from console.anthropic.com, which is paid by usage.

✨ AI score new leads

Claude reads each new lead and gives a 1–10 score, a tag (Individual / Small shop / Agency / Big company / Unrelated) and a one-line reason. Sort by AI score to invite the best first.

🤖 Agent mode

  1. Write a goal: "Find 20 plumbers with mobile numbers in Nallasopara. Skip big showrooms."
  2. Choose the app and a Google call budget (5–30). It never goes over.
  3. Tap ▶ Run agent. It plans, searches, judges, saves and stops by itself.
  4. Watch the log: 🧠 thinking · 🔎 searching · 💾 saved · ✅ done. Tap ■ Stop any time.

Claude cost (estimate)

About ₹1 per agent run with Haiku, roughly ₹50–150 a month for normal use. New Anthropic accounts get some free test credit.

Coming later: Lead Agent, a chat app with its own icon where you talk to the agent like a chat.

13. Backup and export

Leads live only in this phone's Chrome. Clearing Chrome data, uninstalling Chrome or changing phone deletes them.

⬇️ Export CSV

  • Downloads all leads shown by the current filters as a CSV that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Columns: app, country, name, trade, status, invited date, score, phone, address, area, lat, lng, website, rating, map link, and more.
  • Do it once a week and save it to Google Drive, like your Kaarigar admin backup.

Different phone or browser

Each phone or browser has its own database and settings. You need to paste the key again there.

14. Rules to follow

  • Google terms: use the data for your own outreach. Don't sell lists of Google data or build a permanent copy of Google's database. Results come from Google.
  • India DPDP Act: contact businesses only, for a clear purpose, and delete anyone who asks.
  • TRAI: avoid unsolicited commercial calls and SMS to numbers on the DND list. WhatsApp messages should be personal and one by one.
  • WhatsApp: no bulk blasts. Always honour STOP.
  • Selling Lead Finder: sell the software, with each buyer using their own Google and Claude keys. Don't sell the data.

This is general guidance, not legal advice.

16. Quick daily routine

  1. Open Lead Finder from your home-screen icon.
  2. Search 1–2 trades in 1–2 areas (about 2–4 calls).
  3. Look through new cards and Remove unrelated ones.
  4. Send 5–10 WhatsApp invites, one by one.
  5. Update statuses for anyone who replied.
  6. Move Interested people into the app (they register, or you use import lines).
  7. Friday: Export CSV to Google Drive.
10 invites a day is about 300 a month, all within the free Google allowance.
